Cookie Policy

We believe in complete transparency. This policy explains how and why Zynochat uses cookies, local storage, and other technical identifiers to keep your session secure and your interface lightning fast.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone, or other internet-enabled device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, maintain security, and remember your configurations, preferences, and login credentials over time.

In addition to standard cookies, Zynochat uses modern web browser features such as HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage to store configuration states, cryptographic settings, and active user interface tokens securely.

2. What Cookies & Storage We Use

Zynochat categorizes its use of storage technologies into three main classes. We do NOT use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral targeting cookies.

A. Strictly Necessary & Security Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Platform to function. They enable security protocols, prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks, and maintain active login sessions. Disabling these cookies will result in authentication failures and a highly unstable platform.

  • Session Identifiers: Secure JWT and refresh tokens stored in HttpOnly cookies to keep you signed in securely without exposing credentials to JavaScript.
  • CSRF Tokens: Temporary cryptographic tokens used to verify that state-changing API actions originate from our authorized interface.

B. Preference & Personalization Storage

These storage elements are used to remember your interface customization and settings so you don't have to reconfigure them every time you reload.

  • Theme preference: Remembers your chosen UI skin (Dark Mode, Light Mode, or System Default).
  • Chat custom wallpapers: Saves your selected chat background color or custom pattern configuration.
  • Layout settings: Remembers the width of your sidebar panels and active chat list sorting preferences.

C. Performance & Privacy-Preserving Analytics

We use zero-tracker, privacy-first analytics to monitor server performance, detect response latencies, and optimize application speed.

  • Server health diagnostics: Anonymized indicators stored in session state to count page load speeds and API response latencies, helping us maintain sub-millisecond execution.

3. HTML5 Local Storage & Cryptography

Due to our advanced technical stack and secure design, Zynochat utilizes HTML5 Local Storage for local-only storage of cryptographic artifacts.

  • Public Cryptographic Keys: Local storage houses public identity keys used to perform secure key exchanges with other active users.
  • Cached Chat Lists: To deliver instantaneous interface rendering (near-zero latency), we safely cache contact names, display-avatars, and basic metadata locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Cookies and Services

Zynochat is hosted with security-centric backend architectures. However, third-party infrastructure providers might establish cookies to keep APIs functional:

  • Supabase API: Supabase uses secure tokens to facilitate instant database synchronization via WebSockets. These tokens are stored securely in browser local storage and expire automatically.
  • APNS & Firebase Cloud Messaging: Standard browser identifiers to register devices for real-time chat push notifications. No user identities are shared with Google or Apple.

5. How to Manage and Block Cookies

You have the right to accept, configure, or refuse cookies on your device. Most web browsers are configured to accept cookies automatically, but they provide controls to manage this behavior.

  • Browser settings: You can block or delete cookies directly through your browser settings. Check your browser's "Help" or "Settings" panel (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Brave, etc.) for instructions.
  • Strictly Necessary exclusion: Note that if you clear or disable all cookies, you will be unable to log in, access your chats, or utilize the security-hardened features of Zynochat.
  • Local Storage clean-up: You can clear HTML5 Local Storage at any time using your browser's Developer Tools or "Clear Browsing Data" menu.

6. Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our technology or legal requirements. When we publish modifications, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we safeguard your browsing integrity.

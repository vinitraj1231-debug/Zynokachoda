Refund Policy

We believe in straightforward, customer-first pricing. This page details our billing cycles, subscription cancellations, refund eligibilities, and account upgrade terms.

1. Subscription & Billing Terms

Zynochat provides core real-time communication services free of charge. We also offer optional premium subscriptions ("Zynochat Premium") that unlock enhanced AI features, additional group controls, large-scale file sharing (chunk uploads), custom wallpapers, and exclusive badges.

By upgrading to Zynochat Premium, you agree to these billing conditions:

  • Recurring Billing: Premium plans are billed on a recurring basis — either monthly or annually, based on your selection. Billing occurs at the beginning of each cycle using your saved credit card, debit card, or verified online wallet.
  • Automatic Renewal: To ensure uninterrupted service, your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled prior to the renewal date.
  • Price Clarity: All prices are displayed clearly in our billing interface before upgrade. Pricing adjustments may be made with at least 30 days of advance notice via email.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your Zynochat Premium subscription at any time. Cancellation is straightforward and can be initiated directly from your Account Settings.

Upon cancellation:

  • Access Preservation: Your premium features will remain completely active until the final day of your current paid billing period.
  • Auto-Renewal Termination: At the end of that cycle, your account will downgrade to the free tier, and no further automatic charges will occur.
  • Data Retention: Downgrading your account will not delete your chat histories, profiles, or custom configurations, though some premium-specific controls (such as active custom wallpapers or custom badges) will be temporarily deactivated.

3. Refund Eligibility Criteria

We want you to be absolutely satisfied with Zynochat Premium. To provide an honest and reliable experience, we offer refunds on premium plans subject to these guidelines:

  • 14-Day Cooling-off Period: If you are dissatisfied with Zynochat Premium, you are eligible for a full refund if you submit a formal request within 14 calendar days of your initial purchase or annual renewal charge.
  • Technical Issues: If a persistent technical failure on Zynochat's servers prevents you from accessing premium features for more than 48 consecutive hours, we will issue a prorated refund or extend your subscription cycle free of charge, regardless of the 14-day limit.
  • Monthly Renewal Exceptions: For monthly recurring renewals, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. To qualify, you must show that you were unable to access the premium services or that the renewal occurred due to a billing error.

4. Refund Exclusions & Policy Violations

To prevent exploitation, Zynochat denies refund requests under the following conditions:

  • Terms of Service Violations: If your account is suspended, restricted, or terminated due to an explicit violation of our Terms and Conditions (such as distributing spam, harassment, or unlawful content), you forfeit all rights to any refund for active subscriptions.
  • Exploitative Requests: Users who repeatedly subscribe and request refunds across multiple accounts to bypass the subscription limits will have their payment methods permanently blacklisted and refund requests denied.
  • No Usage Justification: Simply choosing not to use the platform after purchasing a subscription does not constitute a valid reason for a refund outside the 14-day cooling-off window.

5. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

We encourage you to contact us directly if you notice any unfamiliar charges on your statement. Initiating a formal chargeback or payment dispute with your credit card company or bank before reaching out to Zynochat support can result in:

  • Automatic Account Suspension: To protect against potential fraud, your Zynochat account may be locked or suspended during the active dispute investigation.
  • Debt Collection: If a chargeback is issued maliciously for services rendered, we reserve the right to recover the funds along with any bank processing fees incurred.

6. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, follow these steps:

  1. Ensure your request meets the eligibility criteria in Section 3.
  2. Email our billing support desk at billing@zynochat.in.
  3. Include your:
    • Zynochat Username
    • Registered Email Address
    • Invoice/Transaction Number
    • Brief explanation of the reason for your refund request.
  4. Our billing department will review your request and issue a response within 3 business days. Approved refunds will be processed immediately and credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 bank business days.

7. Contact our Billing Department

For any queries, invoice requests, or subscription upgrades, please contact our financial support department:

  • Email: billing@zynochat.in
  • Support Desk: zynochat.in/contact
  • Business Inquiries: business@zynochat.in
